IDENTIFICATION

Signs of Tick Infestation

Ticks Crawling Up Walls & Curtains

Brown dog ticks climbing upward along walls, curtain folds, baseboards, and door trims.

Ticks Attached to Dogs & Pets

Engorged, seed-like ticks discovered behind pet ears, between toes, on necks, or under armpits.

Cluster Activity Near Dog Kennels

Concentrations of unengorged flat nymphs hiding in wooden kennel joints, dog beds, and compound walls.

Restlessness in Household Pets

Frequent scratching and agitation in domestic animals resulting from continuous tick activity.

FAQS

Frequently Asked Questions

How do ticks differ from fleas?

Ticks are arachnids (8 legs as adults) that crawl rather than jump. Unlike fleas which stay primarily on animals or carpets, ticks climb vertical surfaces like walls, doorframes, and curtains to molt and lay eggs.

How much does tick control cost in Bangalore?

Tick control at SLV Pest Control starts from ₹799. Final pricing depends on indoor room count, presence of external lawns or kennels, and infestation intensity.

Can ticks survive indoors away from pets?

Yes. The brown dog tick (Rhipicephalus sanguineus) is uniquely adapted to complete its entire lifecycle indoors, surviving in wall cavities for several months without feeding.

Is premise treatment alone sufficient for tick management?

Environmental pest control treats premises, walls, and grounds—it does not treat ticks on animals. While SLV Pest Control manages your living spaces and walls, pets must be checked and treated in consultation with a qualified veterinarian.
