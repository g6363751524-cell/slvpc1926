IDENTIFICATION

Signs of Termite Activity

Inspect your foundation walls, door frames, and wooden fixtures for these characteristic indicators.

Mud Shelter Tubes

Earthen tunnels running along foundation plinths, brick walls, skirting boards, door frames, or ceiling joints.

Hollow-Sounding Timber

Door frames, window jambs, and wardrobe panels that sound paper-thin, dull, or hollow when tapped firmly.

Discarded Swarmer Wings

Piles of shed translucent wings left near windowsills, doors, and light fixtures following seasonal rainy-evening flights.

Tight Doors & Window Jams

Doors or windows becoming difficult to close or latch due to moisture produced by subterranean termite activity inside frames.

PREPARATION GUIDANCE

Before Treatment

Move loose furniture 2–3 feet away from skirting boards and walls.
Clear the base of modular wardrobes where drilling is needed.
Keep door frames and wooden wall paneling accessible for inspection.
Inform the technician of previously observed swarmer flights or tubes.
POST-TREATMENT CARE

After Treatment

Allow drilled holes and aesthetic compound sealant to dry undisturbed.
Avoid wet mopping floor skirting boards with standing water immediately.
Fix any recurring plumbing leaks contributing to structural dampness.
Follow the technician's monitoring instructions and follow-up guidance.

FAQS

Frequently Asked Questions

How does subterranean termite treatment work?

We use non-repellent liquid termiticides injected under pressure into precision-drilled holes along wall skirtings, door frames, and masonry joints. Termites unwittingly pass through the treated zone, transfer the active agent to the subterranean colony, and eliminate the nesting source.

How much does termite control cost in Bangalore?

Termite treatment at SLV Pest Control starts from ₹899. The final price depends on the linear footage of walls to be treated, woodwork volume, construction design, and property layout assessed during the on-site evaluation.

Will drilling damage marble, granite, or wooden flooring?

Our technicians utilize specialized precision drill bits (6mm to 8mm) placed discreetly along wall skirtings and tile joints. Following infusion, all holes are sealed with color-matched aesthetic compound.

Can termites damage electrical wiring?

Yes. Termites frequently use electrical conduits as concealed travel highways and can chew through wire insulation, presenting short-circuit hazards.

Is termite treatment suitable for high-rise apartments?

Yes. Termites routinely travel through central plumbing shafts and masonry joints into upper-floor apartments. Our drill-and-infusion protocol is fully adapted for residential flats.

Why is an on-site inspection required before pricing?

Because termite damage occurs behind walls and beneath floorings, an inspection is necessary to measure linear running footage, check woodwork depth, and evaluate foundation access for an accurate quote.
