COMMON INQUIRIES

Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly can an SLV technician arrive at my property?

We provide prompt same-day service across all major Bangalore corridors. In cases of acute pest emergencies, priority dispatch units can reach your location swiftly.

Are the treatments safe for children, pets, and senior citizens?

Yes. We strictly utilize Central Insecticides Board (CIB) approved botanical and low-toxicity formulations. Our residential applications are odorless, non-staining, and family-friendly.

Do you offer a service warranty or satisfaction guarantee?

Yes. Our specialized treatments (such as Bed Bug Management and Termite Treatment) come with structured service warranties with scheduled follow-up visits.

Do we need to vacate our home during the treatment?

For standard cockroach gel baiting and ant treatments, vacating your home is completely unnecessary. For intensive misting or bed bug protocols, technicians advise a brief settling period.
