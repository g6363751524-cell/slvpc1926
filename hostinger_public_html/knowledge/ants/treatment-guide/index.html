Ant Treatment Guide: 4-Stage Integrated Colony Management

Effective ant control requires a structured Integrated Pest Management (IPM) methodology. Rather than spraying surface chemicals that trigger colony budding, SLV Pest Control follows a disciplined 4-stage management process tailored to Bangalore properties.

Service Standard: SLV Technical Protocol•Bangalore Residential & Commercial•Full Protocol Disclosure

The Science Behind Our 4-Stage Ant Control

Over-the-counter insecticide sprays fail because they only target foraging workers visible on surfaces. Ants communicate via social food sharing known as trophallaxis. When returning workers share slow-acting baits with the queen and nursing workers inside the nest, the entire reproductive engine of the colony is dismantled.

Our treatment follows four clear, accountable stages designed for domestic homes, apartment complexes, restaurants, and corporate pantries across Bangalore:

Our 4-Stage Treatment Journey

Stage 01|

Inspection

Objective: Identify ant activity, likely harbourage and environmental conditions across kitchens, bathrooms, wall gaps, and entry points.

Mapping active foraging trails along floor skirting, tile joints, and kitchen backsplashes.
Checking under-sink plumbing cabinets, drain traps, and kitchen appliances for moisture accumulation.
Inspecting exterior foundation walls, balcony planters, and utility lines for outdoor nesting sources.
Stage 02|

Assessment

Objective: Determine the appropriate management approach based on inspection findings and property conditions.

Evaluating species feeding preferences (sugar-loving vs protein-loving species).
Identifying whether the infestation is a primary nest or multiple budding satellite colonies.
Designing a custom treatment layout safe for households with young children and pets.
Stage 03|

Targeted Treatment

Objective: Apply an appropriate treatment method to suitable areas based on the inspection.

Precision placement of high-palatability gel bait dots along active foraging tracks and cabinet hinges.
Application of targeted dust formulation in inaccessible dry wall voids and conduit openings.
Applying non-repellent perimeter treatments at exterior thresholds where necessary to prevent incoming scouts.
Stage 04|

Follow-up & Prevention Guidance

Objective: Provide monitoring and practical prevention recommendations.

Guidance on maintaining undisturbed bait stations during the critical transfer period (24–72 hours).
Detailed structural sealing and caulking suggestions to close known entry apertures permanently.
Post-service monitoring check to confirm complete cessation of trail movement and colony collapse.
