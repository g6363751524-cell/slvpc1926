Cockroaches

Blattella germanica & Periplaneta americana

Kitchen Threat

Nocturnal scavengers sheltering in warm kitchen crevices, under sinks, and behind appliances. Carry 33+ bacterial species and trigger severe childhood asthma.

Key Identification Markers

  • German: 12-15mm light brown with 2 dark parallel pronotum stripes
  • American: 35-40mm reddish-brown, active night glider
  • Black pepper-like fecal specks along cabinet hinges
  • Foul oily pheromone scent in heavily infested spaces
Common Hiding ZonesBehind refrigerator motor compressors, under sink P-traps, modular drawer runners, and false ceiling gaps.
Bangalore Spread ZoneHigh density across all urban apartments, especially Koramangala, Indiranagar, HSR Layout, and Whitefield.

Subterranean Termites

Coptotermes gestroi & Heterotermes

Zero-Tolerance

Silent wood-destroying insects nesting underground that build earthen mud tubes up building plinths to consume timber, door jambs, and false ceilings from the inside out.

Key Identification Markers

  • Creamy-white soft-bodied workers hidden inside hollowed timber
  • Brown earthen mud shelter tubes branching along masonry plinths
  • Discarded translucent alate wings beneath windows after monsoon rains
  • Woodwork that sounds paper-thin and hollow upon light tapping
Common Hiding ZonesSub-slab soil gradients, door frame plinths, modular kitchen baseboards, and expansion joint mortar voids.
Bangalore Spread ZoneExtremely prevalent in new red-soil suburban layouts (Sarjapur Road, Devanahalli, Kanakapura Road, Electronic City).

Bed Bugs

Cimex lectularius

High Discomfort

Obligate blood-feeding nocturnal parasites that harbor within 5–8 feet of sleeping areas. Extremely resilient to over-the-counter aerosols.

Key Identification Markers

  • Oval, flattened apple-seed shaped reddish-brown bodies (4-5mm)
  • Linear clusters or zig-zag patterns of 3-4 intensely itchy red bites
  • Tiny black digested blood spots along mattress seams and bed sheets
  • Translucent cast exoskeletons shed during five nymphal molts
Common Hiding ZonesMattress piping, box springs, wooden headboard joins, electrical switchplate backs, and sofa seams.
Bangalore Spread ZoneFrequent in transit zones, PG hostels, rented tech corridors, and serviced apartments in Marathahalli, Bellandur, and BTM Layout.

Ants (Black, Carpenter & Pharaoh)

Camponotus compressus & Monomorium pharaonis

Colony Invader

Persistent social insects establishing pheromone highways from foundation soils or cavity walls straight into pantries and electrical sockets.

Key Identification Markers

  • Black carpenter ants (6-12mm) excavating smooth galleries in damp wood
  • Tiny yellow pharaoh ants (1.5-2mm) infesting sterile rooms & appliances
  • Uniform trails marching along floor skirting and countertop splashbacks
  • Fine wood frass accumulation beneath wooden architraves
Common Hiding ZonesWall voids, window sill cavities, behind switchboards, compost bins, and wet plumbing ducts.
Bangalore Spread ZoneWidespread across ground floor villas, tree-adjacent balconies, and high-rise apartment kitchen shafts.

Mosquitoes

Aedes aegypti, Culex & Anopheles

Vector Disease

High-risk flying disease vectors that breed in stagnant water containers, plant saucers, and storm drains. Rest on dark indoor curtains and furniture backs during daylight.

Key Identification Markers

  • Aedes: Distinct black and white lyre body markings; daytime biting
  • Culex: Plain brownish-gray body; nocturnal biting and loud ear buzzing
  • Visible aquatic wriggler larvae squirming in standing water sumps
  • Resting on dark curtains, behind wardrobes, and under desks
Common Hiding ZonesUnder beds, behind hanging window drapes, balcony plant drip trays, and unsealed overhead water tanks.
Bangalore Spread ZoneLake catchment belts and storm water drain perimeters (Bellandur, Varthur, Hebbal, Ulsoor, and Mahadevapura).

Rodents (Rats & Mice)

Rattus rattus & Mus musculus

Wire & Food Hazard

Aggressive gnawers that chew electrical insulation, vehicle engine harnesses, and contaminate food stores. Can squeeze through openings as narrow as 6mm.

Key Identification Markers

  • Spindle-shaped black droppings (6-12mm for rats, 3-6mm for mice)
  • Nocturnal scratching sounds above false ceilings and within drywall
  • Distinct parallel incisor gnaw marks on PVC pipes, doors, and wires
  • Dark greasy smudge trails (sebum) left along wall baseboards
Common Hiding ZonesFalse ceilings, modular kitchen voids, behind refrigerators, AC ducting, and basement electrical shafts.
Bangalore Spread ZoneOlder commercial hubs and restaurant belts: Chickpet, Commercial Street, Brigade Road, Majestic, and Jayanagar.

Structural ThreatHigh Risk

Wood Borers (Powder Post Beetles)

Lyctidae & Anobiidae

Timber Destroyer

Wood-boring beetle larvae that spend years tunneling through seasoned hardwoods, plyboards, and furniture frames, converting timber into ultra-fine flour dust.

Key Identification Markers

  • Tiny circular exit pinholes (1-2mm) appearing on polished furniture surfaces
  • Small conical mounds of talc-like pale yellow wood powder (frass) beneath tables
  • Structural weakness and sudden crumbling along wooden frame edges
  • Small 3-5mm dark brown cylindrical adult beetles emerging in warm weather
Common Hiding ZonesBacking of wardrobes, unlaminated undersides of dining tables, wooden door jambs, and antique timber.
Bangalore Spread ZonePrevalent in vintage colonial bungalows, teak/rosewood collections, and untreated imported plywood furnishings.

Fleas

Ctenocephalides felis & canis

Pet & Carpet Vector

Wingless jumping insects with laterally compressed bodies that jump onto hosts from carpet piles, pet beds, and floor gaps to draw blood.

Key Identification Markers

  • Tiny 2-3mm dark reddish-brown insects capable of jumping 200x body length
  • Bites clustered almost exclusively on lower legs, ankles, and feet
  • "Flea dirt" (peppery black blood specks) in pet fur and along pet bed fibers
  • Pets persistently biting, licking, or scratching their rump and belly
Common Hiding ZonesBase of deep carpet piles, pet sleep cushions, upholstery crevices, and floorboard gaps.
Bangalore Spread ZonePet-friendly residential complexes, independent villas with outdoor turf, and animal shelters across Sahakarnagar and JP Nagar.

Brown Dog Ticks

Rhipicephalus sanguineus

Veterinary Risk

Eight-legged parasitic arachnids uniquely adapted to complete their entire life cycle indoors, crawling up vertical walls and attaching to canines.

Key Identification Markers

  • Flat, teardrop-shaped brown arachnids that swell into gray beans when engorged
  • Seen crawling vertically up white walls, baseboards, and window curtains
  • Attached firmly to dog ears, neck folds, between paw toes, and belly
  • Pet showing lethargy, high fever, pale gums, and loss of appetite
Common Hiding ZonesCracks in compound walls, kennel woodwork, baseboard expansion joints, and curtain header tape.
Bangalore Spread ZoneCanine boarding facilities, gated villa communities, and compound walls across Banashankari and Yelahanka.

Flies (House, Drain & Fruit)

Musca domestica, Psychodidae & Drosophila

Sanitation Threat

High-speed biological vectors breeding in moist decomposing organic matter, unsealed drains, and fermenting kitchen waste, transferring pathogens to food.

Key Identification Markers

  • House flies (6-7mm) with 4 dark longitudinal stripes on thorax
  • Drain flies (moth flies) with fuzzy hairy wings hovering near bathroom floor traps
  • Fruit flies (3-4mm) hovering over overripe bananas and bar taps
  • Dark fly speck defecation spots on light fixtures and upper walls
Common Hiding ZonesUnder rim of trash bins, biofilm lining inside shower floor traps, and damp mops.
Bangalore Spread ZoneCommercial restaurant kitchens, food courts, and apartments near open storm water channels.

DIAGNOSTIC MATRIX

Common Pest Confusion in Bangalore

Property owners frequently misdiagnose early infestations. Use this comparative criteria before choosing a treatment.

Bed Bugs vs. Fleas

Parasite Comparison
Bed Bugs
  • • Cannot jump or fly; crawls slowly.
  • • Bites clustered on torso, arms, neck.
  • • Stays within 6ft of human beds.
  • • Leaves rusty blood specks on sheets.
Fleas
  • • Powerful jumpers (up to 20cm).
  • • Bites concentrated on ankles and feet.
  • • Resides in carpet fibers & pet beds.
  • • Leaves black pepper “flea dirt” in pet fur.

Termites vs. Carpenter Ants

Wood-Inhabiting
Subterranean Termites
  • • Consumes cellulose; leaves wood hollow.
  • • Builds brown earthen mud shelter tubes.
  • • Straight antennae; equal wings on swarmers.
  • • Does NOT produce coarse wood shavings.
Carpenter Ants
  • • Excavates wood for nesting; does not eat it.
  • • Piles visible coarse sawdust (frass) below holes.
  • • Elbowed antennae with pinched waist.
  • • Forages openly across kitchen floors.

German vs. American Cockroach

Cockroach Species
German Cockroach
  • • Small (12–15mm); light tan with 2 dark stripes.
  • • Lives strictly indoors (kitchen cabinets & appliances).
  • • Fast reproduction cycle; carries egg case.
  • • Requires targeted gel baiting, not sprays.
American Cockroach
  • • Large (35–45mm); glossy reddish-brown.
  • • Originates from sewers, drain pipes, sumps.
  • • Can glide across rooms when startled.
  • • Requires drain trapping & perimeter residual barrier.

Rats vs. Mice

Rodent Sizing & Behavior
Roof / Norway Rats
  • • Large body (200–250mm); thick scaly tail.
  • • Blunted or pointed snout; neophobic (cautious).
  • • 12–15mm banana-curved or blunt droppings.
  • • Squeezes through 12mm openings.
House Mice
  • • Tiny body (60–90mm); large ears, thin tail.
  • • Inquisitive and curious foragers.
  • • 3–6mm pointed spindle black droppings.
  • • Can squeeze through tiny 6mm dime-sized holes.
