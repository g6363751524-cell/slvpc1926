Frequently Asked Questions About Tick Control

Clear, factual answers regarding tick behavior, hiding habits, property preparation, inspection protocols, and premises safety across Bangalore.

Q01

How do I know if I have ticks around my property?

Common indicators include noticing live ticks crawling upward along walls, door trims, or curtain pleats; observing seed-like ticks attached to pets (around ears, neck, or paws); and discovering small tick clusters within pet crates, bedding seams, or wooden kennel joints.

Q02

Where do ticks usually hide?

Ticks hide in protected, dark harbourages close to where pets rest. Indoors, they shelter in pet bedding seams, behind baseboards, in vertical wall cracks, and within upholstery folds. Outdoors, they occupy shaded kennel corners, exterior compound wall crevices, and garden margins.

Q03

How do ticks enter a property?

Ticks are typically introduced when domestic pets pick them up from outdoor environments such as neighborhood parks, vegetated footpaths, or contact with other animals during daily walks. Once carried indoors, fed ticks drop off and establish harbourages within building cracks.

Q04

Can pets contribute to tick activity?

Yes. Domestic pets serve as the primary host for the Brown Dog Tick (Rhipicephalus sanguineus). When pets travel through areas with tick presence, they can unknowingly introduce ticks into the household environment.

Q05

How much does tick control cost?

At SLV Pest Control, pricing is provided after inspection. The investment depends on property dimensions, the extent of indoor wall crevices, pet resting areas, and whether outdoor compound perimeters, lawns, or kennels require treatment.

Q06

Is inspection required?

Yes. An initial on-site property inspection is essential. It enables our technician to assess the severity of infestation, locate focal harbourages both indoors and outdoors, and customize the environmental treatment approach to property conditions.

Q07

How long does tick treatment take?

A typical residential premises treatment takes approximately 45 to 90 minutes, depending on the number of rooms, presence of external compound walls, and the scale of pet kennel facilities.

Q08

What preparation is required?

Before premises treatment, clear pet bedding for separate hot laundering, move lightweight furniture slightly away from wall baseboards to allow perimeter access, and keep pets and family members safely outside the specific areas being treated during application.

Q09

What should I do after treatment?

Allow treated surfaces to dry completely before re-entering (typically 1 to 2 hours, as advised by the technician). Continue washing pet bedding regularly, inspect pets after walks, and follow the technician's environmental prevention recommendations.

Q10

Can tick activity return?

Yes. Tick activity can recur if pets re-encounter ticks outdoors in public parks or neighborhood grounds and carry them back indoors, or if unhatched eggs in deep unsealed masonry voids emerge. Continuous monitoring, pet checking, and environmental hygiene help minimize recurrence.

Q11

Do you treat homes and apartments?

Yes. SLV Pest Control provides professional tick management for independent villas, gated community apartments, paying guest accommodations, and rental properties across Bangalore.

Q12

Do you treat outdoor areas?

Yes. Where appropriate and indicated by inspection, we treat external compound perimeter walls, kennel structures, veranda crevices, and shaded paved borders to address outdoor harbourage reservoirs.

Q13

Should pets be checked by a veterinarian?

Yes, absolutely. Environmental pest control treats premises, walls, and structures—it does not treat ticks on living animals. We strongly advise pet owners to consult a qualified veterinarian for appropriate on-pet treatments and preventive health recommendations.
