Recognize early signs of mosquito activity, understand aquatic breeding conditions, and locate outdoor resting zones around your property.

Understanding Mosquito Biology & Activity Signs

Mosquitoes are flying insects whose life cycle is intrinsically linked to stagnant water. Females require protein from a blood meal to develop viable eggs, which they deposit directly onto or near standing water surfaces. Within 24 to 48 hours, these eggs hatch into aquatic larvae, completing their transition into flying adults in as few as 7 to 10 days in Bangalore's warm, humid climate.

Because adults can fly several hundred meters from their original breeding source, identifying mosquito activity requires assessing both immediate indoor harbourages and outdoor standing-water reservoirs within the perimeter.

IDENTIFICATION SIGNS

Key Indicators of Mosquito Activity

Persistent Biting Indoors & Balconies

Frequent bites occurring during dawn and dusk hours (or during daylight hours from container-breeding Aedes mosquitoes) across balconies, verandas, living spaces, and ground-floor corridors.

Visible Larvae (Wigglers) in Standing Water

Small, active worm-like larvae wriggling near the surface of stagnant water in flowerpot saucers, AC condensation trays, bucket bottoms, and open cisterns.

Audible High-Pitched Buzzing at Rest

Persistent high-pitched whining or buzzing sounds around bedrooms, study desks, and quiet corners, causing disrupted sleep and heightened bite awareness.

Resting Adults in Shaded Vegetation

Adult mosquitoes clustered under dense garden foliage, ornamental shrubbery, hanging planters, balcony privacy screens, and beneath outdoor stairwells during the heat of the day.

Activity Near Wet Drainage Lines

Elevated swarm activity concentrated around stagnant stormwater drains, inspection chambers, washing area gulley traps, and basement sump collection pits.

Indoor Daytime Wall Harbourage

Adult mosquitoes resting motionless on dark indoor walls, behind full-length window curtains, under beds, and inside wardrobe gaps to escape direct ceiling fans.

Common Mosquito Categories in Bangalore

Container Breeder

Aedes Mosquitoes

Distinct black-and-white lyre markings on the thorax. Day-active biters peaking in early morning and late afternoon. Breeds in clean, artificial water containers such as plant saucers, discarded tires, and open water tanks.

Biting habit: Daytime outdoors & balconies
Drain & Sewage Breeder

Culex Mosquitoes

Brownish, robust bodies. Nocturnal biters active after dusk and throughout the night. Proliferates in organic-rich, stagnant drainage channels, wastewater ditches, septic tanks, and polluted stormwater corridors.

Biting habit: Nighttime indoors & bedrooms
Vegetation & Sump Breeder

Anopheles Mosquitoes

Rests at a characteristic 45-degree angle to the surface with spotted wings. Breeds in clean, slow-moving water sources, shaded overhead tank covers, and garden ponds with natural vegetation.

Biting habit: Dusk to dawn
