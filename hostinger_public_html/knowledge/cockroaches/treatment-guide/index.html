A disciplined, scientific Integrated Pest Management (IPM) approach combining forensic inspection, precision odorless gel baiting, and sustained monitoring.

Why Generic Sprays Fail Against Cockroaches

Over-the-counter pesticide sprays only kill the small percentage of foraging insects exposed to direct misting. Because the active ingredients in household sprays are irritating repellents, they trigger a biological scatter response, driving surviving cockroaches deeper into wall cavities, false ceilings, and adjoining rooms.

SLV Pest Control employs an Integrated Pest Management (IPM) strategy centered on targeted, odorless gel baits and non-repellent formulations. Cockroaches feed on the palatable bait, return safely to their harbourage, and transfer the active ingredient through grooming and social sharing—effectively controlling the population at its source without contaminating living spaces.

IPM WORKFLOW

Our 4-Stage Treatment Protocol

01

Inspection & Activity Mapping

Identify activity and likely harbourage areas across the property.

A certified SLV Pest Control technician conducts a thorough visual and diagnostic assessment of kitchen cabinetry, appliances, drain traps, plumbing sleeves, and storage areas. The inspection determines the species involved (German vs. American), pinpointing focal harbourage zones and the infestation severity level.

Phase 01 of 04
02

Targeted Treatment

Treat suitable areas according to the inspection findings and property conditions.

Treatment is tailored specifically to your premises. We utilize professional-grade, odorless gel baiting placed precisely in secluded cracks, crevices, hinges, and appliance motor housings away from food-contact surfaces. Where appropriate for perimeter or drainage control, targeted non-repellent residual barrier applications are applied along entry thresholds and plumbing ducts.

Phase 02 of 04
03

Monitoring & Follow-up

Monitor activity and provide appropriate follow-up guidance.

Because cockroach eggs (oothecae) hatch within 2 to 4 weeks depending on ambient humidity, ongoing monitoring is essential. Our team provides structured guidelines to observe nymph emergence, evaluate bait consumption, and schedule follow-up assistance as required by property conditions.

Phase 03 of 04
04

Prevention Guidance

Provide practical recommendations to help reduce future activity.

Following treatment, the technician delivers property-specific advice on moisture mitigation, structural caulking, waste management, and delivery parcel inspection to help minimize the risk of re-introduction from external vectors.

Phase 04 of 04

How Odorless Gel Baiting Works

Cockroach gel baits contain complex food attractants (sugars, proteins, lipids) paired with slow-acting active ingredients. When applied as tiny dot matrices (the size of a peppercorn) in secluded hinges and crack recesses:

1. High PalatabilityAttracts foraging nymphs and adults away from competing food residues.
2. Delayed ActionAllows the insect sufficient time to return undisturbed to its deep harbourage.
3. Horizontal TransferShared within the nest through coprophagy and necrophagy, targeting hidden nymphs.

Practical Treatment Expectations

Because eggs enclosed in oothecae are biologically impervious to chemical contact, single-day elimination does not reflect biological reality. Complete management requires allowing sufficient time for existing egg capsules to hatch and for newly emerged nymphs to contact the active gel placements. Professional pest control relies on sustained IPM principles, not unrealistic guarantees.
