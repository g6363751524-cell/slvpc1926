Flea Control FAQ

Clear, factual answers to 13 common questions regarding flea biology, environmental treatments, vacuuming protocols, and veterinary collaboration in Bangalore.

How do I know if I have fleas?

Common indicators include itchy red bite clusters around your ankles and lower legs, observing tiny reddish-brown insects jumping across rugs or furniture, pets scratching or biting at their fur persistently, and discovering black pepper-like specks (flea dirt) on pet bedding that dissolve reddish on damp paper towels.

Where do fleas usually hide?

While adult fleas live on animal hosts to feed, eggs, larvae, and pupal cocoons hide deep within carpet piles, pet cushions, upholstery seams, underneath low furniture, along timber floorboard cracks, and in shaded outdoor resting areas.

Can pets contribute to flea activity?

Yes. Domestic dogs and cats frequently bring fleas indoors after picking them up during walks, from dog parks, or from interaction with other animals. Because female fleas lay non-sticky eggs directly on pets, these eggs roll off as pets walk, spreading throughout the home.

How do fleas spread?

Fleas spread when animal hosts transport adult fleas into indoor spaces. Eggs then fall onto carpets and bedding, where larvae feed on organic flea dirt, spin protective silken cocoons, and emerge as hungry adult jumpers in response to host footsteps and body warmth.

How much does flea control cost in Bangalore?

Flea control pricing is confirmed after an on-site property inspection. The cost depends on the square footage of carpeted and upholstered areas, number of rooms requiring environmental treatment, severity of the infestation, and whether outdoor compound zones require treatment.

Is an inspection required before treatment?

Yes. An on-site inspection allows our technicians to identify active flea hotspots, map out pet resting areas, check for secondary pest vectors (such as rodents or stray animal access), and calibrate an appropriate treatment protocol.

How long does flea treatment take?

Environmental application typically takes between 1 to 2 hours depending on the property size. Residents and pets are usually asked to vacate the treated premises for 2 to 3 hours during and immediately following treatment until surfaces have completely dried.

What preparation is required before treatment?

Property owners should thoroughly vacuum all carpets, rugs, and upholstered furniture (disposing of the vacuum contents outdoors), pick up clutter and pet toys from floors, and launder all pet bedding in hot water (60°C or above).

What should I do after treatment?

Wait until treated surfaces are thoroughly dry before re-entering. Continue to vacuum carpets and rugs every 2 to 3 days for the next 2 weeks. The vibrations encourage remaining pupae in cocoons to emerge and contact the residual treatment barrier.

Can flea activity return after treatment?

Yes. Fleas can re-occur if untreated pets continue to carry newly acquired fleas indoors from outdoor spaces, or if dormant cocoons hatch without follow-up vacuuming. Ongoing preventative hygiene and coordination with your veterinarian are vital.

Do you treat residential homes and apartments?

Yes. SLV Pest Control provides comprehensive environmental treatments for independent houses, villas, and apartments across Bangalore, targeting carpet piles, rug underlays, and furniture bases.

Do you treat areas where pets spend time?

Yes. Our environmental treatments pay particular attention to pet sleeping corners, rugs, sofa bases, and shaded balcony verandas where pets rest. However, we do not apply pest control products directly onto animals.

Should pets be checked by a veterinarian?

Yes, absolutely. Environmental pest control treats your premises, but treating the animal host requires qualified veterinary care. We strongly advise having your pet examined by a licensed veterinarian who can recommend safe, veterinary-approved flea preventative medications.
